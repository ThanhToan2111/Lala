## Positioning sentence

Existing methods primarily optimize a fused representation or decompose task information after choosing a multimodal objective. ConFu++ instead asks, under explicit representation and hypothesis-class conditions, whether the target modality contains measurable non-additive predictive structure, whether the downstream task has corresponding joint headroom, and whether a distilled interaction remains faithful and accessible.
